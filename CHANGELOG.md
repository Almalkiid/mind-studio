# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First prototype.

- Open any folder, note or the whole vault as a mind map, from the file menu, the ribbon or the commands.
- Folders, notes and headings become branches; a folder holding one note shows as that note, and a note's first
  `# Title` names it. Numbered names show a short tag ("Unit 03", "3a").
- A soft colour per main branch, in light and dark mode.
- Branches on both sides on a desktop; one side, folded, on a phone or a narrow pane. Big maps stop zooming out at a
  readable size.
- Tap a topic to select it and tap again to open it (a note at that heading, or a folder as the new centre); fold
  buttons; header buttons to open, go up a folder and fit.
- The map follows changes in the vault and keeps what was folded; typing in a note does not redraw it, renaming the
  mapped folder or note is followed, and a pane resized across the phone width switches layout.
- A topic opens at its own heading, even when two headings share a name. Up skips a folder that only holds the mapped
  note; back returns to the previous map. Dragging the map from a card does not open it, and fold buttons have a
  fingertip-sized tap area that tolerates a small wobble.
